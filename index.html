Clear answers to
common questions.

Yes, absolutely! Teeth can be moved safely at almost any age as long as your gums and bone structure are healthy. Many of our adult patients choose clear aligners or discreet ceramic braces for subtle, effective alignment.
Clear aligners are custom-engineered, transparent plastic trays that fit tightly over your teeth. Each tray applies calibrated micro-pressure to move teeth incrementally. You swap to a new tray every 1-2 weeks as guided by Dr. Prudhvi.
Treatment duration depends on the complexity of your bite. Mild alignment cases can take 6-9 months with clear aligners, while comprehensive orthodontic cases typically range between 12 and 18 months. We provide an exact estimated timeline during your initial evaluation.
Modern dentistry prioritizes comfort. For orthodontic adjustments, you may feel mild tightness for a few days after new wires or aligners. For restorative procedures, localized anesthesia ensures a virtually painless experience.
During your first visit, Dr. Prudhvi performs a comprehensive dental examination, discusses your primary concerns, takes digital photographs or scans if required, and outlines transparent treatment options tailored to your schedule and budget.
