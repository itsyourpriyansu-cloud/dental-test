Clear aligners are custom-made transparent trays that gradually move teeth. They are removable and discreet, but they need to be worn as instructed for effective results.
Treatment duration depends on the case. Mild alignment concerns may take around 6–9 months, while more complex bite or crowding cases may take 12–18 months or longer. Your timeline will be explained after evaluation.
Most modern dental procedures are designed around comfort. Some treatments may cause mild pressure or sensitivity, but Dr. Prudhvi will explain what to expect before treatment begins.
